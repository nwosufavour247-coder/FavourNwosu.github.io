About Me

In today's digital environment, organisations face growing threats to their networks, systems and sensitive information. Effective cybersecurity practices are essential for protecting digital assets, maintaining business continuity and building trust.

I am Favour Nwosu, a cybersecurity enthusiast and information security professional with an interest in helping organisations identify vulnerabilities, manage security risks and strengthen their digital environments.

My areas of interest include penetration testing, vulnerability assessment, security monitoring, information security, data privacy, governance, risk and compliance (GRC), and network security.

I continuously develop my technical and analytical skills through practical projects, cybersecurity labs and professional training.
